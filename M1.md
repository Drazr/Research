# M1: probing methodology for G9-Q6 and G7

Prepared 14 November 2026. Companion to [research.md](research.md) and [paper.md](paper.md).

This is a question-focused methodology shortlist from **category 1 (38 papers) and category 3 (15 papers)**. All 53 references receive a disposition: **19 core method references, 19 potentially useful supporting references, and 15 background/not-shortlisted references**. The first reading pack contains eight core references; the remaining core references address specific methodological needs. These are reading priorities, not 19 required methods or 53 proposed experiments.

**S01-S53 are local M1 reference IDs.** They follow the combined category-1/category-3 catalog order and are unrelated to the P01-P42 benchmark-review numbering in analysis.md. Each reference is linked to a primary paper. Source PDFs were retrieved; abstracts, relevant methodology sections and limitations were checked for the distinctions below. This is not a line-by-line review of every appendix or a fresh systematic novelty survey.

**Attribution rule.** “Published method” summarizes the source. “Dataset-only adaptation” is our proposed use or modification, not something the source necessarily did or a claim of novelty. “Controls,” “assumptions” and “limits” identify requirements for a defensible adaptation. Only non-interventional diagnostic components are retained. The category-2 methodology file is reserved for M2; it is not created here.

## 1. Ongoing questions and their separate roles

G9 and Q6 are grouped for planning, but their targets remain distinct. G7 is the connected reporting question. All retain the same no-new-manual-work boundary as research.md.

### G9. How are category discrimination, cross-language readout compatibility and confidence reliability related under released caption contexts?

Integrated question: relate category discrimination, transfer compatibility and confidence reliability. A presence probability and a probability of answer correctness are different objects.

### Q6. Can cross-language readout incompatibility coexist with accessible image-category information under the released native-caption contexts?

Focused question: distinguish source-readout portability from within-context category accessibility. Geometry is a possible account, not an established explanation.

### G7. Can joint reporting of the released categories become unreliable while individual category information remains accessible?

Reporting question: relate category accessibility to a structured category report. Joint category presence is not physical object count, and reporting format is not semantic correctness.

## 2. Dataset and no-new-manual-work contract

The semantic targets come from the released XM3600 images, original captions and shared root-category annotations: [XM3600](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.45/), [annotation paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.478/), [annotation CSV](https://github.com/uriberger/cross_lingual_diff_in_descs/blob/main/data/xm3600_annotation.csv), and [repository/loader](https://github.com/uriberger/cross_lingual_diff_in_descs#object-mentions-dataset).

- **Existing visual gold only:** 3,600 images and 25 broad, shared image-presence categories. The same image-category target applies across English, Hindi, Bengali and Telugu. There are no per-language visual labels to translate. Counts, regions, attributes, subtype identities, relations and cultural facts are not supplied by these roots.
- **Original native captions only:** they can provide language-conditioned context without newly translated prompts or manually verified paraphrases. They are independently written, not equivalent translations. Same image does not mean same caption content. The target is the observed image-plus-caption context, not a controlled language-only treatment.
- **No fresh human validation or dataset construction:** no new assertion labels, native terminology checks, answer judgments, bilingual fidelity checks, image adjudication, OCR annotation or new image/caption corpus. An external benchmark's labels or a human-audited translation set do not become available because a method paper used them.
- **Automatic processing is allowed:** joining existing IDs, tokenizing, extracting frozen features, defining image-group partitions, deterministic label/format scoring, fitting diagnostic readouts and computational development-set selection. “Validation” below means numerical model selection on released data, not human verification. This does not remove implementation or compute costs.
- **Reporting uses the released label space:** a fixed mapping to the existing category identifiers can define machine-readable decisions and deterministic correctness. That mapping is a task convention, not a newly validated Indic lexicon. A common interface across caption contexts does not test native instruction understanding or native-language answer quality. A formal ID alone has no inherent semantic meaning for a VLM; its association with the released category must be explicitly represented by the common interface.
- **No free-text gold from another model:** LLM judgments, automatic translations, semantic clustering and mention extraction are not treated as independently verified truth. Category-ID correctness uses the CSV; free-caption hallucination, native assertions and full semantic fidelity remain outside M1.
- **Fallible released labels remain fallible:** the previously audited plant cell with value `4` is unknown, not a count or an automatically repaired label. A deterministic validity mask can exclude invalid cells without viewing images. Blank cells follow the release convention. Categories with very few positives do not acquire adequate statistical support through repeated captions.
- **No intervention component:** no activation patching, steering, erasure, model editing or base-model fine-tuning is required. Training a diagnostic probe or transforming already extracted features inside that readout is permitted and must be distinguished from changing the VLM.

The Bengali/Telugu warning concerns derived translation-dependent mention files, not original captions or the shared CSV. M1's ongoing presence/reporting questions do not need those derived mention targets. All four languages therefore remain within scope.

## 4. Core methodology cards

These cards are reusable methodological ingredients. They deliberately do not specify experiment grids, model choices, sample sizes, training budgets or a sequence of competing-explanation tests.

### S01. [Understanding intermediate layers using linear classifier probes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.01644) (2016)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** G9; Q6; G7.

**Method location:** Linear classifier probe definition and layer-wise analysis. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.01644).

**Published method:** Fit independent linear classifiers to frozen intermediate representations. Their held-out predictive performance characterizes accessibility to that readout family at each layer.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Use the existing 25 binary category targets, with category-specific logits or a multilabel linear head. The same target definitions can support within-context and cross-context readouts.

**Assumptions:** Enough independent positive and negative images exist for the category and the fitted readout is adequately estimated.

**Controls to carry over:** Keep base-model weights frozen; separate image identities across data partitions; match feature extraction, training support and probe capacity. Keep the representation position and pooling rule explicit.

**Limits / do not import:** Linear inaccessibility does not establish absence of information. High accuracy does not establish that the model uses the decoded feature. It also does not identify whether the feature came from the image or its accompanying caption.

### S02. [Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/) (2019)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** G9; Q6; G7.

**Method location:** Control tasks and selectivity. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275.pdf).

**Published method:** Contrast a linguistic probing task with a control task assigning random labels to word types, to expose a probe's capacity to memorize lexical identities.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Carry over the control-task principle. An automatically shuffled image-level target vector is a useful leakage/memorization diagnostic using the existing collection; it is an adaptation, not the paper's word-type control.

**Assumptions:** The control is matched to the artifact being investigated, rather than merely easy to fail.

**Controls to carry over:** Keep all captions/languages for an image together. Preserve the chosen control's class statistics and use identical probe capacity and preprocessing. A caption-text baseline addresses lexical predictability separately.

**Limits / do not import:** Chance performance on random labels alone does not rule out semantic shortcuts, pretraining memorization, caption leakage or excessive probe capacity. Do not call a simple image-label shuffle the original selectivity test.

### S24. [Response Wide Shut? Surprising Observations in Basic Vision Language Model Capabilities](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1241/) (2025)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** G7 primary; G9; Q6.

**Method location:** Section 3: visual, projection and response-space evaluation. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1241.pdf).

**Published method:** Probe frozen visual-encoder, vision-language projection and language-decoder features, and separately evaluate generated responses. The paper uses pooled representations and task-specific linear classifiers to compare stages.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Retain stage-specific category readouts and compare them with automatically scored category-ID outputs. This supports G7's accessibility/reporting distinction using the released root labels.

**Assumptions:** The chosen open model exposes comparable stages; architecture-specific fusion may require a different stage inventory.

**Controls to carry over:** Match the image sets and category targets across stages; make pooling and token timing explicit. Keep format failures separate from incorrect category decisions and retain shuffled-label controls.

**Limits / do not import:** Their task datasets are not our annotation source. A gap between independently trained probes and generated answers does not alone prove information loss or a particular cognitive failure. Decoder states obtained after generation have a different information budget from pre-answer states.

### S05. [Encoded but Not Routed: Explaining the Table-Chart Gap in Scientific Claim Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01679) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** G7; Q6.

**Method location:** Linear Probing and Attention Analysis in the methodology. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.01679).

**Published method:** Compare layer-wise binary probes on mean-pooled input states and the final input-token state. A claim-text-only control tests evidence-free prediction; the paper separately examines attention to visual tokens.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Use category-presence targets to distinguish distributed accessibility from accessibility at a reporting position. Existing image and caption inputs permit evidence-source baselines without new semantic annotations.

**Assumptions:** Different positions expose meaningfully different accessible information under the chosen model's attention mask.

**Controls to carry over:** Keep probe capacity and data units comparable; separate pooling breadth from representational quality. Use image-group separation rather than copying its small-claim leave-one-out procedure mechanically.

**Limits / do not import:** Our images lack semantically matched table/chart evidence, and native captions are not equivalent statements. A pooled-versus-last-token gap is a positional accessibility observation, not proof of causal routing. Attention weights alone do not establish evidence use.

### S47. [HALP: Detecting Hallucinations in Vision-Language Models without Generating a Single Token](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.287/) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** G9 confidence; G7.

**Method location:** Section 3: HALP representation families and pre-generation probing. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.287.pdf).

**Published method:** Predict generation risk from visual-only features, decoder visual-token states and query-token states before any response token is produced. The paper compares layers and architectures.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Its feature inventory and pre-generation timing are directly useful. Any correctness target here must be derived from a fixed-space category answer and the released presence vector, not an unannotated free caption.

**Assumptions:** The structured task and parser define answer correctness without human interpretation; appropriate internal states are accessible.

**Controls to carry over:** Distinguish predicting category presence from predicting the correctness of a model decision. Keep risk labels disjoint from features used to fit their predictor, and respect architecture-specific token types.

**Limits / do not import:** The original hallucination tasks provide supervision that XM3600 does not. No pre-generation hallucination claim for arbitrary Indic captions follows. Risk predictability is not calibrated reliability unless measured separately.

### S33. [Shared Doubt: Zero-Shot Cross-Lingual Confidence Estimation for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31220) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 3: Probing + cross-language transfer. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** G9 primary; Q6; G7 correctness.

**Method location:** Sections 3 and 4.1: layer-weighted correctness probe and evaluation metrics. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.31220).

**Published method:** Learn a softmax-weighted mixture of layer states, followed by a linear sigmoid correctness predictor. Compare last-query and last-answer features and transfer a source-trained predictor across languages; evaluate discrimination and calibration separately.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Category-answer correctness can be computed from released labels, allowing a correctness readout without free-text judging. A category-presence head is a different target and must remain separately named.

**Assumptions:** Layer coordinates support the proposed mixture and the correctness event is stable across contexts.

**Controls to carry over:** Correctness labels used for fitting must come from held-out or cross-fitted decisions. Layer mixing, score calibration and thresholds need declared development data. Separate AUROC/AUPR from Brier/log-loss and descriptive calibration plots.

**Limits / do not import:** The original parallel QA and answer-evaluation resources are not imported. Target-fitted calibration is adaptation, even though existing labels make it automatic. Source-trained layer weights do not establish a universal shared confidence mechanism.

### S39. [Cross-Lingual Transfer and Shared Geometry of Deception Probes in LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2026.mrl-1.33/) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 3: Probing + cross-language transfer. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** G9; Q6.

**Method location:** Sections 3.2 and evaluation/geometry analyses. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2026.mrl-1.33.pdf).

**Published method:** Transfer language-specific logistic probes, compare signed directions in a common raw activation basis, and summarize a shared direction by SVD. Standardized coefficients are divided by their training feature scales before directional comparisons.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Use shared category presence as the positive class. Compare functional transfer with geometric alignment of category directions; any consensus direction is built from declared source languages only.

**Assumptions:** Directions refer to the same model, feature basis and target. Cross-model coordinates are not automatically comparable.

**Controls to carry over:** Keep signs consistent, preprocessing source-fitted, and basis transformations explicit. Assess direction stability and absolute discrimination; a small transfer penalty can preserve a weak baseline.

**Limits / do not import:** The original deception prompts, behavioral judgments and construct-validity labels are unavailable and unnecessary for this adaptation. High cosine similarity is neither reliable monitoring nor causal evidence. A consensus built using a held-out target language is not source-only transfer.

### S31. [A Shared Geometry of Difficulty in Multilingual Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-short.66/) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 3: Probing + cross-language transfer. **Priority:** Start here. **Question links:** Q6 primary; G9.

**Method location:** Sections 3.1 and 3.3: common item splits, layer-wise probes and transfer grids. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-short.66.pdf).

**Published method:** Train layer-wise ridge regressors for continuous problem difficulty and compare within-language and cross-language prediction. The paper keeps problem identities together across translations and uses development data for regularization.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Carry over the distinction between best within-context accessibility and cross-context portability. Replace difficulty regression with category-presence classification using the released labels; this changes the target and loss.

**Assumptions:** Sufficient category support exists to compare layers fairly.

**Controls to carry over:** Image identities replace problem identities as grouping units. A source-only selection rule and a target-development-selected rule must have different transfer labels; do not choose the peak layer from test performance.

**Limits / do not import:** Its human difficulty scores, translated parallel problems and qualitative translation checks are outside scope. Native XM3600 captions do not supply meaning equivalence. Spearman correlation for difficulty is not a substitute for category discrimination or calibration.

### S06. [How Multimodal LLMs Solve Image Tasks: A Lens on Visual Grounding, Task Reasoning, and Answer Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20279) (2025)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** Q6; G7.

**Method location:** Section 2: anchor-question probes and prompt variations. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.20279).

**Published method:** Train fine-grained visual-category probes on last-token states under an anchor question, then keep the probe fixed under lexical, semantic and answer-format changes.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** The useful principle is separating a changed reporting interface from a changed diagnostic readout. Existing root-category labels and untouched native captions support fixed-space accessibility comparisons.

**Assumptions:** A probe transferred across input conditions still has a well-defined target and feature position.

**Controls to carry over:** Declare the original paper's model-compliance filtering when interpreting its conclusions. Our adaptation cannot silently discard difficult images based on model answers or assume all categories are present.

**Limits / do not import:** The original all-positive animal anchor, dog-breed targets and meaning-controlled rewrites do not come with XM3600. Do not synthesize Indic paraphrases and treat them as verified equivalents. Its grounding/reasoning/decoding stage interpretation is not established automatically for our models.

### S04. [Responses Fall Short of Understanding: Revealing the Gap between Internal Representations and Responses in Visual Document Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04411) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G7; G9.

**Method location:** Layer-wise linear probing versus generated responses. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04411).

**Published method:** Decode document-task answers from intermediate LVLM states and compare their recovery with generated-response accuracy; the paper also studies intermediate-layer fine-tuning.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Retain the diagnostic distinction between a readout-accessible category decision and a structured generated report. Both can use the same released root-category targets.

**Assumptions:** The generation task has a deterministic label-space interpretation; no human document-answer verification is borrowed.

**Controls to carry over:** Keep the target, evaluated image population and information available to each method aligned. Separate pre-answer states from answer-conditioned states and distinguish per-category from whole-report correctness.

**Limits / do not import:** Only the observational probing component is in M1. Document QA annotations, intermediate-layer fine-tuning and claims that a representation-response gap proves understanding are not transferred. This paper is close prior work, so observing a generic probe/output gap alone is not a new contribution.

### S23. [The First to Know: How Token Distributions Reveal Hidden Knowledge in Large Vision-Language Models?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09037) (2024)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G7; G9.

**Method location:** Section 3: linear probing on first-token logit distributions. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.09037).

**Published method:** Fit diagnostic classifiers to the full distribution at the first generated-token position and compare it with distributions at later generation steps; the paper studies several answerability and reliability tasks.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** A first-position logit vector is an additional readout feature for existing category targets or automatically determined structured-answer correctness.

**Assumptions:** Full logits are accessible, and the first output position is consistently defined for the chosen task.

**Controls to carry over:** Differentiate a learned classifier over the logit vector from taking the probability of a category's token. Keep vocabulary dimension, answer format and temporal information budget explicit.

**Limits / do not import:** Do not use single-token probabilities as equivalent native category scores when names have different tokenizations. The paper's answerability labels and decoding interventions are not imported. Later-step information may include the model's own prior outputs rather than better original visual access.

### S11. [Information-Theoretic Probing with Minimum Description Length](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.14/) (2020)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G9; Q6.

**Method location:** Section 2: variational and online/prequential codelength. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.14.pdf).

**Published method:** Measure label-description length, incorporating predictive quality and the cost or data-efficiency of learning a probe. Online coding learns from preceding blocks and codes subsequent labels.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Description length can complement accessibility accuracy using the existing category labels. Whole-image groups, not individual captions, must define coding blocks.

**Assumptions:** The coding procedure and units are the same across the representations being compared.

**Controls to carry over:** Respect prefix-only fitting, matched ordering/support and a declared initial code. Compare against an appropriate class-prior code; keep multilabel independence assumptions explicit.

**Limits / do not import:** A fixed source probe's target-language log-loss is conditional predictive coding, not automatically a full target-learned MDL estimate. Coding all category bits independently does not quantify their joint dependence. Results remain finite-data, probe-family-dependent evidence, not an absolute inventory of model knowledge.

### S44. [Pareto Probing: Trading Off Accuracy for Complexity](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.254/) (2020)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G9; Q6; G7.

**Method location:** Accuracy-complexity Pareto evaluation and complexity metrics. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.254.pdf).

**Published method:** Treat probe performance and complexity as separate objectives, comparing Pareto frontiers rather than selecting one model family or its best score unconditionally.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Use the released category targets to compare linear, reduced-rank or modest nonlinear readouts. This can bound how dependent an accessibility conclusion is on diagnostic model capacity.

**Assumptions:** Complexity values are comparable under the chosen feature scaling and architecture.

**Controls to carry over:** Match training support, preprocessing and tuning budgets. State the complexity measure; parameter count, norms and description length are not interchangeable. Use unchanged test data for final comparisons.

**Limits / do not import:** A stronger nonlinear probe does not show that the base model uses a nonlinear computation for that category. Sophisticated structured detectors should not be preferred solely because they score better on this small, imbalanced collection. Its dependency-parsing task is not part of our data.

### S16. [Probing the Probing Paradigm: Does Probing Accuracy Entail Task Relevance?](https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-main.295/) (2021)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G9; Q6; G7.

**Method location:** NLI and controlled-task analyses of decodability versus task relevance. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-main.295.pdf).

**Published method:** Show that linguistic properties can remain decodable even when they are unnecessary for a model's trained task, and investigate contributions from pretrained representations.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Use its interpretive boundary when connecting category readouts to structured reporting. Existing image/caption inputs permit separate evidence-source baselines without adding semantic annotation.

**Assumptions:** Readout and reporting outcomes are kept as distinct measured objects.

**Controls to carry over:** Distinguish recoverability, predictive association with output errors, and causal use. A caption-only classifier can reveal that presence is predictable without vision; it is not a caption-mention annotation.

**Limits / do not import:** The paper's controlled synthetic linguistic tasks are not reproduced. A successful category readout cannot certify native-language comprehension, and an observational probe/output discrepancy cannot alone identify the mechanism that caused a report error.

### S17. [Subspace Chronicles: How Linguistic Information Emerges, Shifts and Interacts during Language Model Training](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.879/) (2023)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G9; Q6.

**Method location:** Task-specific information subspaces and principal subspace angles. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.879.pdf).

**Published method:** Analyze information-bearing probe subspaces and compare their organization using principal angles, accounting for differing ranks and arbitrary basis orientation within a subspace.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Existing category labels can define category-relevant subspaces across native-caption contexts. Their overlap is complementary to held-out readout transfer.

**Assumptions:** The ambient coordinate system is comparable; subspace estimates are supported by enough independent images.

**Controls to carry over:** Express weights in a common feature basis; report subspace rank and stability. For a single category direction, preserve present-positive orientation separately from unsigned subspace angles.

**Limits / do not import:** Do not infer a training trajectory from a single frozen checkpoint or claim that neuron/subspace overlap causes transfer. Geometry can agree even when predictive usefulness differs. Training-checkpoint analyses in the original work are not a requirement here.

### S34. [Examining Cross-lingual Contextual Embeddings with Orthogonal Structural Probes](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.376/) (2021)

**Source category:** Category 3: Probing + cross-language transfer. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** Q6 primary; G9.

**Method location:** Section 3: language-specific orthogonal maps and task scaling. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.376.pdf).

**Published method:** Factor structural probing into orthogonal language transformations and task-specific scaling vectors, using monolingual syntactic and lexical structure labels to investigate shared encoding geometry.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Borrow the constrained-factorization idea for category readouts, replacing structural losses with existing presence targets. This is a proposed category-probing model, not a replication of their structural objective.

**Assumptions:** An orthogonal map is a defensible approximation; poorer transfer can also reflect content or accessibility differences.

**Controls to carry over:** Separate an unchanged source readout from target-supervised projection fitting. Fit any transformation only on declared development/training groups and compare its capacity with an unconstrained alternative.

**Limits / do not import:** UD trees, native lexical hypernymy labels and parallel captions are not supplied. Presence supervision does not validate syntactic isomorphism. Image-matched captions are weak visual anchors, not meaning-equivalent sentence pairs, and geometry correction alone is not causal explanation.

### S30. [Probe Generalization as Subspace Selection for OOD Deception Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02893) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** Q6; G9.

**Method location:** Source-activation PCA and target-guided component selection. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02893).

**Published method:** Study source-domain PCA components as a feature basis for transfer. A target-labeled greedy component search gives an oracle comparison; a separate branch uses an LLM to interpret/select components.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Retain source-fitted PCA and explicit distinctions between source-only selection and a target-development-supervised diagnostic upper comparison, using released category labels.

**Assumptions:** Reduced dimensions retain relevant category signal and the selected subspace is stable with limited data.

**Controls to carry over:** Keep PCA fitting, component selection and final scoring image-disjoint. A target-guided subset is not zero-shot. High variance and large source-probe weights do not by themselves identify transferable features.

**Limits / do not import:** No deception judgments or LLM semantic interpretations of components enter M1. Target test labels cannot select components. The source paper's target-guided performance does not establish a source-only improvement that we can directly inherit.

### S32. [LLMs Encode Relevance as a Layer-Wise Cross-Lingual Signal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15555) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 3: Probing + cross-language transfer. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G7; Q6; G9.

**Method location:** Section 3: internal versus generated relevance decisions and multilingual portability. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.15555).

**Published method:** Probe last-token residual states for relevance, compare probe-based predictions with generated judgments, and evaluate same-language and cross-language portability.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Carry over the separation of internal-readout decisions and explicit model judgments. The shared category labels replace retrieval-relevance annotations and keep scoring automatic.

**Assumptions:** The fixed-space reporting task permits a meaningful comparison to category readouts.

**Controls to carry over:** Use the same binary category definition across contexts and separately account for invalid generated formats. Make the source/target training and layer-selection policy explicit.

**Limits / do not import:** Our data has no query-document relevance or retrieval-system gold. Its relevance pseudo-labeling and system-ranking results cannot validate caption factuality here. This is methodological inspiration, not an assertion that relevance and visual presence share a mechanism.

### S29. [Lingua Franca or Probing Artifact? Rethinking Latent Language in Multilingual LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.504/) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Priority:** Read when needed. **Question links:** G9; Q6; interpretation of G7.

**Method location:** Representation-based versus decoding-based latent-language identification. [Primary full text](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.504.pdf).

**Published method:** Contrast representation-based language identification with raw/tuned vocabulary-decoding routes and show that their layer-wise language estimates can disagree systematically.

**Dataset-only adaptation:** Treat readout choice as part of the measurement claim. Existing caption-language metadata supports an auxiliary language-ID diagnostic without native semantic annotations; category accessibility remains a separate target.

**Assumptions:** Language-ID estimates characterize a particular estimator and input distribution.

**Controls to carry over:** Keep token position, pooling, normalization and output-head assumptions visible. A vocabulary-decoding preference should not replace the category readout or behavioral transfer measurement.

**Limits / do not import:** No automatically decoded word or external LID judgment is accepted as proof of the model's mental language. Native category verbalizers are not verified by this dataset. This paper supplies an artifact warning, not a ready-made causal test of an English pivot.

## 5. Potentially useful supporting methods

These stay available for a specific methodological need. None requires its original external dataset or annotation process to be imported; only the bounded contribution stated below is retained. A conditional reference is not an additional proposed research question.

### S03. [MedProb: Probing Internal Representations of Vision-Language Models for Medical Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04336) (2026)

**Source category:** Category 1: Probing. **Question links:** G7.

**Methodological contribution:** Predict multiple-choice answers from frozen VLM features and examine answer-position effects.

**Possible fit:** The category-ID reporting space can supply existing targets; no medical data is needed for that diagnostic adaptation.

**Boundary:** Multiple-choice answer selection is not the same task as multilabel reporting. Retain position/format cautions; do not import medical claims, rejection-sampling generation or external medical QA annotations.

## 7. Cross-paper synthesis for later experiment design

These are combinations of ideas worth carrying forward, not completed experimental protocols or claims that the combinations are novel.

1. **Capacity-aware category accessibility:** S01 provides the readout; S02 supplies the control-task caution; S44 and S11 help distinguish accessibility from probe capacity and learning effort. This preserves Q6's readout-relative meaning. Random-label failure is one check, not complete proof of validity.
2. **Functional transfer with geometry as separate evidence:** S31 separates within-language and cross-language behavior; S39 supplies direction comparison and a warning about absolute baseline quality; S17/S34 offer subspace or constrained-map tools. Improvement after a fitted map can be compatible with a coordinate explanation, but is not uniquely diagnostic of one.
3. **Confidence attached to a defined decision:** S33 supplies a correctness-readout framework; S47 distinguishes pre-generation feature locations; S23 offers a logit-vector alternative. Any new learned confidence target is derived from fixed category decisions and existing labels. These ingredients cannot certify free-caption hallucination.
4. **Accessible information versus structured reporting:** S24/S04 motivate comparing independent category readouts with model reports; S05 adds position/pooling distinctions. G7's gold is the existing multilabel vector. A mismatch can also involve task-interface demands, thresholds, output order, report length or formatting; it is not yet a verified routing bottleneck.
5. **Measurement-route sensitivity:** S29 and supporting S46 caution against inferring an English internal language or reliable transfer from a decoding lens or a geometric similarity alone. They support interpretation checks; they do not supply missing native-word semantic ground truth.

## 8. Requirements that any later design must retain

### Data units, leakage and selection

Every caption and language view of an image shares its visual target and image identity. Training, computational development and test partitions must therefore be disjoint by **image ID across all four languages**. Caption count is not independent image count. Unequal numbers of captions must not silently give some images or languages more weight. Layer, token site, regularization, normalization, PCA and any score/threshold selection must have a declared fitting partition.

Existing target-language labels make a target-trained comparator or calibration procedure possible without manual work. They do **not** make that procedure source-only zero-shot transfer. Target test labels are for final scoring, not map, component, layer or threshold selection. Input-only target normalization is a separate transductive choice and should be named if used. Source-to-target comparisons require the same held-out image identities, not exposure to those images through another language during fitting.

Uncertainty estimates for differences must also respect image grouping: repeated captions/languages from one image are not independent resampling units. Layer/category searches require declared selection and multiple-comparison handling; the largest observed gap is not automatically a reliable finding. A fold containing only one label class has an undefined AUROC, not an AUROC of zero. Sparse-category conclusions need their actual support and uncertainty, not pooled precision borrowed from common categories.

### Presence, confidence and correctness

For category k, a presence score estimates `P(y_k = 1 | features)`. Confidence in a chosen binary decision is a different quantity: for a threshold-0.5 decision, `max(p, 1-p)` is its implied correctness confidence only under that probability interpretation. A separately fitted correctness head predicts the event `decision == released_label`; it is not another name for the presence head. Correctness examples used to fit a second head must be held out or cross-fitted relative to the decision system.

Rank discrimination, thresholded decision quality and probability reliability need separate evidence. AUROC does not certify calibration. Average precision depends on prevalence. Proper scores such as Brier score or log-loss complement category discrimination; ECE alone can be misleading for sparse or imbalanced categories. Class-prior/constant-output references are necessary context. Class weighting or resampling changes the training distribution, so a sigmoid trained that way is not automatically calibrated to the release's prevalence. Both category support and the definition of any macro/micro summary must remain visible.

These are our dataset-specific reporting requirements, consistent with the source distinctions in S33/S39, not numerical results from those papers.

### G7's reporting interface and information budget

The only supported semantic answer space is the released category identifiers and their binary/set interpretation. The interface association must be specified consistently; it cannot assume that an opaque ID is understood naturally or that native category names are verified. Output-format compliance, valid-but-wrong category decisions and whole-set correctness are distinct outcomes. Report omissions are errors only for an explicitly exhaustive category-reporting task, not ordinary caption omissions. A broad root label does not identify a particular instance or subtype.

Unknown/invalid label cells cannot be silently turned into absent categories. Full-set correctness needs a declared known-cell scope or automatic exclusion of incomplete target vectors, with the resulting population change stated. Invalid model outputs must not be silently discarded and the remaining conditional accuracy presented as overall reporting reliability. Format coverage, conditional semantic correctness and any explicitly defined end-to-end task-failure event have different denominators. An invalid string does not supply a semantic category decision for a correctness probe.

A single-category decision and a joint report have different output lengths and potentially different error accumulation. Exact-set accuracy naturally decreases as more decisions must be correct; that decrease alone does not establish weaker representations. Per-category outcomes and the existing number of present categories provide needed context without new instance labels.

Pre-response states, states conditioned on the model's own generated answers and states teacher-forced with gold answers have different information budgets. Gold-answer-conditioned accessibility cannot be presented as evidence that the information was independently available before the answer. A category probe and the model's reporter must be evaluated against the same released target, rather than training one to imitate the other and calling agreement truth.

### Caption and modality confounds

The original captions vary in content, length, wording and provenance. A difference across their languages is an observational context difference. Image matching alone cannot convert it into a translation-controlled effect. Caption-only predictability is not a category-mention label, and high image-plus-caption decodability cannot localize its source to vision. Image-only features before text fusion may be identical across language arms and are not four independent language-conditioned representations.

The available labels permit automatic modality and feature-source diagnostics, but no unsupported causal localization or verified caption-content matching. A claim about a pure language penalty, loss of visual knowledge, native instruction comprehension or cultural mismatch would exceed the current annotation-free scope.
